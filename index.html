The full mastery path

Expert-level Japanese takes approximately 2,200–3,000 hours. There are no shortcuts — but there is a right sequence. These six stages map that sequence precisely, from your first hiragana to reading native literature.

30 min/day → fluent in 3–4 years
2 hrs/day + immersion → 18–24 months
