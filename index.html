STAGE 01
入門

▸ JLPT N5 ENTRY · MONTHS 1–2

Build the phonetic foundation of the entire language. Master Hiragana and Katakana — the two phonetic scripts that unlock pronunciation, reading, and 70% of modern Japanese text. Learn survival vocabulary and your first sentence patterns.

46 Hiragana 46 Katakana 100 Vocab Basic Greetings Particles は/が/を
Progress0%
Outcome: Read any hiragana/katakana text aloud. Introduce yourself, ask directions, order food, count to 10,000.
STAGE 02
基礎
KISO · FOUNDATION
▸ JLPT N5 · MONTHS 2–4

Construct your first real sentences. Learn 100 essential Kanji, master verb conjugation (dictionary → て-form → た-form), and build the grammar skeleton that supports all future learning. Introduce past, present, and request forms.

100 Kanji 300 Vocab Verb Groups て-form Adjective types N5 Grammar
Progress0%
Outcome: Pass JLPT N5. Hold a basic conversation. Describe daily routines in present and past tense.

Expand dramatically. Reach 500 Kanji, 1,000 vocabulary, and master intermediate grammar — conditionals, potential form, causative, passive. Begin real comprehensible input: graded readers, easy anime with Japanese subtitles.

THE METHODOLOGY
Seven Core Principles
繰
PRINCIPLE 01
Spaced Repetition First
Every word and kanji is reviewed at precisely the moment you're about to forget it. Anki SRS is non-negotiable at every stage. 20 new cards/day, minimum. Compound retention beats cramming by 400%.
入
PRINCIPLE 02
Comprehensible Input (i+1)
You acquire language by understanding messages slightly above your current level. Krashen's Input Hypothesis: grammar is absorbed unconsciously through massive exposure — not memorized from tables.
音
PRINCIPLE 03
Phonetics Before Literacy
Hiragana and Katakana must be fully automatized before studying kanji. Solid phonetic foundations prevent mispronunciation habits that are extremely difficult to unlearn later.
毎
PRINCIPLE 04
Daily Consistency Beats Intensity
30 minutes every day outperforms 4 hours every weekend. Language learning is a biological process — the brain needs repeated, spaced exposure over time. Streaks are sacred.
話
PRINCIPLE 05
Output Accelerates Acquisition
Speaking forces you to retrieve, not just recognize. Start speaking from Day 1 — even badly. Weekly italki sessions from Stage 2. The discomfort is the learning signal.
楽
PRINCIPLE 06
Enjoyment Sustains Effort
Language learning takes years. Your enjoyment of Japanese content — anime, manga, games, music — is not a distraction. It is the fuel. Optimize your input for what genuinely excites you.
測
PRINCIPLE 07
Measure Everything
Track vocab count, kanji count, study hours, comprehension %. What gets measured gets improved. Set weekly targets. Take the JLPT at each milestone — external pressure creates real deadlines.
