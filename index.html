Progressive learning system

The methodology
Seven core principles
繰
Principle 01
Spaced Repetition First
Every word and kanji is reviewed at precisely the moment you're about to forget it. Anki SRS is non-negotiable. Compound retention beats cramming by 400%.
入
Principle 02
Comprehensible Input (i+1)
You acquire language by understanding messages slightly above your current level. Grammar is absorbed unconsciously through massive exposure — not memorized from tables.
音
Principle 03
Phonetics Before Literacy
Hiragana and Katakana must be fully automatized before studying kanji. Solid phonetic foundations prevent mispronunciation habits that are extremely hard to unlearn.
毎
Principle 04
Daily Consistency Beats Intensity
30 minutes every day outperforms 4 hours every weekend. Language learning is a biological process — the brain needs repeated, spaced exposure over time. Streaks are sacred.
話
Principle 05
Output Accelerates Acquisition
Speaking forces retrieval, not just recognition. Start speaking from Day 1 — even badly. Weekly sessions from Stage 2. Discomfort is the learning signal.
楽
Principle 06
Enjoyment Sustains Effort
Language learning takes years. Your enjoyment of anime, manga, games, music is not a distraction — it is the fuel. Optimize your input for what genuinely excites you.
測
Principle 07
Measure Everything
Track vocab count, kanji count, study hours, comprehension %. What gets measured gets improved. Set weekly targets and take the JLPT at each milestone.
