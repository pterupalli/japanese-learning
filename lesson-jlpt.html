Strategy
How to approach the JLPT
01
Target N5 first — it's reachable in 3–6 months
Complete Stage 1 in this system and you'll have covered the N5 curriculum. Take the JLPT N5 as your first concrete milestone.
02
JLPT is offered twice a year — July and December
Register early (usually 3 months before). Test locations in many countries. Check jlpt.jp for official dates and your nearest venue.
03
Use official practice tests for the final month
JLPT Sensei and the official JLPT website have free practice tests. The format matters — get familiar with time limits and question types.
04
N2 is the professional benchmark for Japan
Most Japanese companies require N2 for non-native employees. If your goal is working in Japan, N2 is the target. This system takes you there.
