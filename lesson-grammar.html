Sentence structure
Japanese word order
Japanese is SOV — Subject Object Verb. The verb always goes at the end.

English: I eat sushi. (S + V + O)
Japanese: 私は寿司を食べます。(S + は + O + を + V)

The subject is often dropped when it's obvious from context. Particles tell you the role of each word — that's why you can rearrange words and still be understood.
