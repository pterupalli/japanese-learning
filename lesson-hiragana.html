How to use this lesson
01
Click any character to open the study popup

02
Start with the vowel row: あ い う え お
These 5 sounds underpin every other character. Every row uses one of these vowels — master them first.
03
One row per day — don't rush all 46 at once
10 rows × 5 characters. One row per day = 10 days to complete. That pace allows proper consolidation.
04
Mark Mastered only when you recall instantly
+5 XP is awarded once. Honest tracking beats inflated numbers. Use Learning while still reviewing.
