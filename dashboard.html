Step 1 of Japanese literacy
What is Kana?
Japanese has two phonetic alphabets — Hiragana and Katakana — each with 46 characters.

Unlike English letters, each character represents a whole syllable sound. Once you know all 92, you can read and write any Japanese word phonetically.

Hiragana (あいうえお) is used for native Japanese words and grammar.
Katakana (アイウエオ) is used for foreign words and loanwords.
