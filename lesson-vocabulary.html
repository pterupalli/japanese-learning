Method
How to build vocabulary that sticks
01
Use the Vocabulary section in the dashboard
Each word has a ▶ Play button for native audio, a YouTube link for pronunciation context, and a CYCLE button to move it through New → Learning → Known.
02
Learn words in sentence context, not isolation
Don't memorize 水 = water. Memorize 水を飲みます (I drink water). Context locks words into long-term memory faster.
03
Mark "Known" only when you can recall instantly
+10 XP is earned the first time you mark a word known. Don't rush — honest tracking beats inflated stats.
04
Add to Anki for spaced repetition
The dashboard vocab list tells you what to study. Use Anki (free) to review daily. 10 new words per day = 800 words in 80 days.
